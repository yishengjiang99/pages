Inventor Claims Machine Will "Talk To Every Home"
Skeptics Call Wireless Picture-Box "A Parlor Novelty At Best"

Demonstration Draws Crowd of Thousands

Before an assembly of gentlemen of science and no small number of curious citizens, the inventor exhibited a cabinet of walnut and glass which, he asserted, would one day carry moving images across the continent. "Within a century," he declared, "every man shall carry such a device in his waistcoat pocket."
The device, as sketched by our artist
The laughter which followed this remark was considerable. A professor of physics was heard to say that the thing would never catch on, as "people have better things to do than stare at a glass rectangle all day."

Weather

Fair and cool. Light winds from the west. Fog in the evening, followed by more fog.

Markets

Wheat steady. Copper firm. Radio shares climb on rumour; brokers advise caution, then buy.
